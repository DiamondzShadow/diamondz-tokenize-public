How it works

From asset to investor in five steps

01

Structure

Pick the asset class, legal wrapper and offering exemption. Upload the documents.

02

Verify

Investors pass KYC, AML and accreditation checks. Results become on-chain claims.

03

Issue

Deploy the token with its compliance rules. Admin keys go to the issuer's multisig.

04

Distribute

Rent, royalties and interest are paid to holders pro rata, in stablecoins.

05

Trade

Transfers between verified holders, or through a licensed trading partner.

Payments

Money in, money out, on the rails people already use

Investors pay by card, bank transfer or stablecoin. Holders receive rent, royalties and interest in the stablecoin or bank account they choose.

Card checkout

Investors buy with a debit or credit card. Card details are handled by the payment processor and never touch the platform.

VisaMastercardApple PayGoogle Pay

Stablecoins

Pay and get paid in the dollar and euro stablecoins that major card networks already settle in, on the chain you prefer.

USDCUSDGPYUSDEURC

Bank transfer

ACH and wire for larger tickets, converted to stablecoin on arrival and back to dollars on payout.

ACHWireSEPA

Automatic distributions

Income is split pro rata across verified holders on a schedule, with a statement for every payout.

MonthlyQuarterlyOn event
Coming soon

Spend payouts by card

Holders spend stablecoin distributions anywhere cards are accepted, through a card-network stablecoin partner.

Institutional · coming soon

Tokenized bank deposits

Settle large subscriptions in bank-issued deposit tokens, which US regulators treat as deposits, not stablecoins.

Investor pays by card, bank or USDC→KYC check passes→Token minted to wallet→Income arrives→Paid out pro rata
